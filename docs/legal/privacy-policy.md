## 4. Personal data we collect

We collect only the data needed for the purposes described in section 5.

### 4.1 Account and profile (all signed-in users)

- **Mobile number:** required to sign in using a one-time password (OTP). Stored encrypted.
- **Name:** your display name, if you provide it.
- **Email address:** optional. Stored encrypted.
- **Preferences:** language (English or Hindi), theme (light, dark or automatic) and notification preferences.
- **Account role:** whether you are a customer, vendor, service provider or rider.
- **Sign-in records:** OTP requests and attempts (the OTP itself is stored only as a one-way hash and expires quickly), and session records.

### 4.2 Addresses and location

- **Saved addresses:** label (for example "Home"), address text, landmark, PIN code, city and area.
- **Precise location (optional):** if you allow the device location permission, we use your current location to find nearby shops and services, sort results by distance, check whether a shop delivers to you and place an address pin on the map. Location is used only when you tap a location action. It is never required, and you can always type an address or choose your area instead.
- **Approximate location:** the city and area you select, used to show relevant listings.

### 4.3 Orders, payments and enquiries (customers)

- **Orders:** items, quantities, prices, delivery or pickup choice, delivery address, order status history, cancellations, refunds and disputes.
- **Delivery OTP and pickup code:** generated and verified on our servers to confirm handover. They are never shown to anyone other than you.
- **Payments:** online payments (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets) are processed by our payment partner **Razorpay**. We receive payment status, amount, a transaction reference and the payment method type. **We do not collect or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password.** For cash on delivery or pay-at-pickup, we record the payment status.
- **Service enquiries:** your name, phone number, message and preferred date, sent to the service provider you contact. Guests can also send enquiries.

### 4.4 Content you create

- Ratings, reviews and review replies
- Comments, likes, hearts, follows and bookmarks
- Reports you submit about listings, reviews, videos or comments
- Messages you send to our support team
- Photos you upload (for example product, gallery, logo or cover images)
- Links to YouTube or Instagram videos that you add (we do not accept video uploads)

### 4.5 Vendors and Service Providers

- **Business profile:** business name (English and Hindi), description, categories, address, map location, business hours, delivery and pickup settings, business phone, WhatsApp number, email and social media links.
- **Verification documents (optional):** owner identity proof, business photo and registration document (for example GST certificate or shop licence). These are stored **privately** and are visible only to you and to authorised ApnaTown admins who review business applications. They are never shown publicly.
- **Billing profile:** details needed for subscription and promotion invoices, such as legal name, billing address and GST number, if you provide them.
- **Business activity:** subscriptions, promotions, offers, orders received, enquiries received and aggregate statistics such as profile view counts.

### 4.6 Riders

- **Application details:** full name, city and area, vehicle type and vehicle registration number.
- **Verification documents:** a selfie, driving licence, identity proof, vehicle registration certificate (RC) and any other document requested. These are stored **privately** and are visible only to you and to authorised ApnaTown admins who review rider applications.
- **Payout details:** UPI ID and/or bank account details, used only to pay your earnings. They are stored encrypted and shown back to you only in masked form.
- **Live location while on duty:** when you switch yourself **Online**, we collect your device location at regular intervals so that we can offer you nearby delivery jobs and so that the customer and shop can track an active delivery. **We do not collect your location while you are Offline**, and your last location is cleared when you go Offline.
- **Delivery records:** jobs offered, accepted and completed, distance, earnings, handover confirmations and disputes.

### 4.7 Technical and security data

- **Cookies and similar storage:** see section 10.
- **IP address, browser or app version and request logs:** used to keep the Platform secure, prevent fraud and abuse (for example OTP abuse and rate limiting), and troubleshoot errors.
- **Aggregate view counts:** when a business profile, offer or video is viewed, we count the view using a temporary, anonymised session value that is deleted within 48 hours. We do not build visitor profiles.

### 4.8 Service-area waitlist

If you ask to be told when ApnaTown launches in your city, we store your mobile number (encrypted) and the city you selected.

### 4.9 Data we do not collect

- We do not read your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, calendar or other files on your device.
- We do not record audio and do not use your microphone.
- We do not collect your location in the background for customers, vendors or service providers.
- We do not use advertising identifiers and do not include third-party advertising or advertising-tracking tools.
- Your recent searches are stored only on your own device and are not sent to our servers.

## 5. How we use your personal data

We use personal data only for the following purposes:

- **Providing the Platform:** creating and securing your account, signing you in with OTP, showing nearby listings, and saving your preferences.
- **Orders and delivery:** placing and splitting orders by shop, calculating prices and delivery fees on our servers, sharing order details with the shop and the assigned rider, live delivery tracking, delivery OTP and pickup code verification, cancellations, refunds and dispute resolution.
- **Payments:** processing payments and refunds through Razorpay, paying riders, and issuing invoices for subscriptions and promotions.
- **Enquiries:** passing your enquiry to the service provider you contacted.
- **Onboarding and verification:** reviewing vendor, service provider and rider applications, including identity and document checks, to keep the marketplace safe and trustworthy.
- **Communications:** sending service messages such as OTPs, order updates and account notices; and, only with your consent, WhatsApp updates and promotional messages.
- **Community and moderation:** publishing reviews, comments and approved videos, and handling reports of abuse.
- **Safety, security and fraud prevention:** detecting and preventing fraud, OTP abuse, fake reviews, unauthorised access and violations of our Terms of Use; keeping audit logs of admin actions.
- **Improving the Platform:** using aggregate, non-identifying statistics (such as view counts and order counts) to improve features and show businesses how their listings perform.
- **Legal compliance:** meeting tax, accounting, consumer protection and other legal obligations, and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not use your personal data for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you without human review. Rider and business applications are reviewed by a person.

## 6. Legal basis for processing

Under the DPDP Law we process your personal data on the following grounds:

- **Consent:** for optional processing such as WhatsApp messages, promotional messages, use of precise device location, and optional verification documents. Consent is requested through a clear notice and can be withdrawn at any time (section 12).
- **Legitimate uses:** where you voluntarily provide data for a specific purpose and have not said that you do not consent to that use (for example, giving your address and phone number to place an order or send an enquiry), and where processing is required to comply with law, a court order or a lawful request.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it. If you withdraw consent for data that is essential to a feature, you may no longer be able to use that feature.

## 8. How we share personal data

We share personal data only as described below. **We do not sell or rent personal data.**

### 8.1 With other users, to complete what you asked for

- **Customer to shop or service provider:** when you place an order or send an enquiry, the business receives the details needed to serve you, such as your name, phone number, delivery address and order or enquiry details.
- **Customer to rider:** the assigned rider receives your first name, delivery address and drop location. Calls between riders and customers use a masked contact option where available.
- **Rider to customer and shop:** during an active delivery, the customer and shop can see the rider's display name, rating, vehicle and live location.
- **Public content:** business profiles, product and service listings, ratings, reviews (with your display name), comments, approved videos and follower counts are public and can be seen by anyone, including guests and search engines.

### 8.2 With service providers who process data on our behalf

These providers act on our instructions, are bound by contract to protect your data, and may use it only to provide their service to us:

- **Razorpay:** payment processing and refunds
- **MSG91:** delivery of OTP and transactional SMS
- **WhatsApp Business (Meta Platforms):** WhatsApp messages, only if you opt in
- **Email delivery provider:** service and promotional emails, only where applicable
- **Cloudinary:** storage, resizing and delivery of public images such as product, gallery, logo and cover images. Private verification documents are **not** stored with Cloudinary.
- **Cloud hosting and storage provider:** hosting our servers, database and private document storage in India

### 8.3 Maps and embedded content

- **Maps:** our maps use OpenStreetMap. When a map is shown, your device loads map tiles directly from OpenStreetMap servers, which receive your IP address and the map area being viewed. See https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
- **YouTube and Instagram videos:** reels and videos are embedded from YouTube or Instagram. When you play an embedded video, those services may collect data under their own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy

### 8.4 For legal reasons and business transfers

- With government authorities, courts, law enforcement or regulators when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or ApnaTown.
- With a successor entity if ApnaTown is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy.

## 11. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes in this policy or as required by law, and then delete or anonymise it.

- **Account and profile data:** while your account is active. After you delete your account, we delete or anonymise it within 30 days, except data listed below that we must keep.
- **Saved addresses:** until you delete them or delete your account.
- **OTP records:** OTP codes expire within minutes; related sign-in attempt records are kept for up to 1 year for security and fraud prevention.
- **Orders, payments, refunds and invoices:** for 8 years from the end of the relevant financial year, as required by tax and accounting laws (including the Companies Act, 2013 and GST laws). These records are kept linked to the minimum identifying information required.
- **Vendor, service provider and rider verification documents:** while the business or rider account is active, and for up to 3 years after it is closed or the application is rejected, to handle disputes, fraud investigations and legal claims, unless a longer period is required by law.
- **Rider payout details:** until you change them or your rider account is closed, and then deleted, except payout transaction records kept with financial records.
- **Rider live location:** only the latest location is kept while you are Online and it is cleared when you go Offline. Locations linked to completed deliveries are kept with the delivery record.
- **Service enquiries:** up to 1 year after the enquiry was last updated.
- **Reviews and comments:** while they are published. When you delete your account, they are deleted or anonymised so they no longer identify you.
- **Support messages and reports:** up to 2 years after they are resolved.
- **Consent records:** for as long as the consent is in effect and for up to 7 years afterwards, to demonstrate that consent was obtained.
- **Security, access and audit logs:** at least 1 year, as required by the DPDP Rules, 2025 and directions of CERT-In.
- **Waitlist entries:** until we launch in your city and notify you, or until you ask us to remove them.

If you have not used your account for 3 years, we may notify you and then delete your account, unless the law requires us to keep the data.

## 14. How we protect your data

- All data sent between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS).
- Mobile numbers, email addresses and rider payout details are encrypted in our database. OTPs and session tokens are stored only as one-way hashes.
- Verification documents are kept in private storage and are accessible only to you and authorised admins.
- Admin access is limited by role, assigned city or area and module permission, and every admin action that changes data is recorded in an audit log.
- We use rate limits, OTP attempt limits and abuse detection to protect accounts.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as required by the DPDP Law, and report cyber security incidents to CERT-In where required.
