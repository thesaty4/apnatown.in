# BE: payments

Features: F-CK-006, F-CK-007, F-OR-006, F-VD-017, F-PV-008; gated Account integration F-AC-001, S-CUST-24 | Rules: BR-01, BR-02, BR-03, BR-20 | State machines: ST-payment, ST-refund | Depends on / used by: cart-checkout, orders, delivery, businesses, settings; `offers` consumes paid-promotion events. Cross-module access is through typed contracts/events only.

Frontend counterpart: [FE payments](../fe/payments.md) · [Feature inventory](../../../docs/FEATURE_INVENTORY.md)

## Responsibility

Own payment attempts, verified financial outcomes, pre-order compensation, refund execution and vendor billing projections. Integrate through `PaymentProvider`; provider/capture topology remains gated. Cart/Checkout orchestrates placement; Orders authorizes refund decisions; Offers alone owns promotion placement.

Use [foundation](../../_foundation/backend-shared.md) **flat** errors `{code,message_key,status,details,correlationId}`, CSRF/session guards and allow-listed DTOs. Money is `bigint` paise in storage and safe-integer paise **numbers** in JSON; all intermediates use shared integer math and reject unsafe serialization. No credentials/raw provider payloads in APIs, logs, analytics or events.

## Rules and use-cases

`PaymentAdminReadPort.metrics` implements the exact scope-first [Admin contract](../../admin/be/admin.md#required-owning-module-ports). Use the same immutable financial facts/policy versions as owner earnings; unknown GMV/commission definitions are null with `unavailable`, not fabricated totals or a private-ledger export.

1. Compute payable amounts from the checkout quote or configured plan/promotion price. Accept only identifiers/methods, never client prices, commission, payout or refund amounts (BR-01).
2. Keep ST-payment: pending → paid/failed, cash pending → collected, fully returned paid/collected → refunded. Retry creates a new pending attempt in a fenced round; it does not erase the old attempt or prove an unknown capture failed. Browser return is not proof of payment.
3. Keep one authoritative per-shop payment allocation for each order (BR-03). A physical `payment_charges` row may fund one/many allocations without double counting; validate allocation sum against charge funding. Lock both allocation and shared charge before reserving any refund/compensation budget. Q-21 chooses topology, not whether duplicate funding is safe.
4. Verify webhook signatures before parsing business data; deduplicate provider event IDs and validate provider/attempt/amount/currency against the stored request. Never log raw payloads, credentials or full payment details.
5. Refund request/decision is not execution. Only Orders' configured authorized decision, committed with `refund.approved`/`refund.declined` through the ports above, changes the request decision; `refund.escalated` follows source ST-refund. Reserve funds in that UoW so concurrent approvals cannot each spend the same remaining balance. Missing authority/amount policy leaves request pending with policy unavailable. Provider failure leaves approved + processing failed, **never** declined/completed. Only verified provider/cash-return evidence permits completion; no admin “mark refunded”.
6. Emit the refund/payment events through the transactional outbox. Orders/Notifications handle the BR-20 customer/admin/vendor notices; Payments does not send duplicate notices.
7. Cash remains pending until explicit verified handover collection. Delivery calls `recordCashCollection` in the same UoW as code consumption/order handover for platform, self-delivery and pickup. Lock financial version/current payable; a stale job COD snapshot is not charge authority. Exactly one collection key per handover; no duplicate collection on retry.
8. Use current configured plan/promotion products, live `payments_online`, approved business and executable activation policy. Snapshot price/configuration/period on purchase; replay preserves it. Retry after a changed/expired configuration requires re-review, not a silent new charge. Renewal is a separately verified purchase; no automatic renewal or proration is introduced (Q-74).
9. Featured listing eligibility is a read of the current Premium subscription effective at the requested UTC instant; Payments remains authoritative. Missing subscription data/policy is unavailable, not active. Product/Service selected flags are not payment or placement records, and selecting a listing never initiates a purchase. After expiry, retain owner selections but make effective featuring false; a verified renewal automatically restores effective featuring for still-selected, otherwise-eligible listings without a listing write.
10. Provider cancellation records `cancellation_requested_at` against the current subscription and keeps its paid entitlement through the immutable `ends_at`; it does not call the payment provider, issue a refund, or end featuring early. A missing `ends_at` makes cancellation unavailable rather than guessing an end date. No vendor cancellation action is added by this provider-specific screen.
9. Ledger sale/commission posting requires confirmed fulfilment, funding and approved recognition/commission basis. `order.status_changed` to delivered/picked_up plus `payment.succeeded`/`payment.cash_collected` supply eligibility; read Orders' financial snapshot by version. Refund debits come from `payment.refund_completed`; purchase entries from verified purchase. Stable source keys prevent duplicates; neither placed orders nor requested/approved refunds are settled money. Unknown Q-06/Q-75 policy gives unavailable projections, not seeded KPIs/payouts.
10. Resolve the vendor from the authenticated session on every read/write. A vendor cannot read another business by changing an ID. Admin financial access is not exposed here; any future admin action needs role + assigned city/area + module permission and audit logging (BR-12).
11. Compensation for captured-but-unplaced checkout is a separate technical return obligation, not a customer refund requiring an order/approver. Persist it outside the rolled-back placement UoW; lock/reserve remaining funding and use a stable provider operation key. Approved adapter policy selects void/refund; unknown operation/outcome stays reconciliation_required. Online checkout is unavailable if no executable compensation/reconciliation is configured (Q-01/Q-21/Q-73).
12. Provider success for a closed/superseded/expired round records the actual capture and publishes `payment.late_capture_detected` for compensation; it cannot bind to a newer round or COD order. Ignore stale failure after verified success; never ignore real late funds just to satisfy an enum. Reconcile anomalies against charge facts while preserving the original logical attempt history.
13. Sum completed + in-flight refund/compensation amounts ≤ funded allocation **and** captured shared charge. Reserve/commit before outbound provider call, then apply verified outcome in another transaction; never hold DB locks across provider I/O. Timeout keeps budget reserved until authoritative reconciliation. Partial refunds remain disabled under Q-76; when approved, numeric funded/returned/outstanding fields must reconcile without relabelling a partially returned payment as fully refunded.

For each verified finite subscription, schedule a delayed `subscription.expire-due` pg-boss check from the committed outbox event using a stable key based on subscription ID and immutable `endsAt`. At the end instant, re-read the current subscription row/version and entitlement at that exact instant; emit `business.subscription.expired` through the outbox only if Premium was no longer continuous. A renewal effective at the boundary suppresses the expiry event; a later renewal does not erase a real gap. Duplicate jobs/outbox delivery cannot duplicate notices. Cancellation leaves the scheduled check and original `endsAt` unchanged.

Jobs: bounded provider calls, refund/compensation retry, subscription expiry checks and webhook reconciliation. Retry the same provider key; terminal/unknown failure stays observable, not silently dropped or auto-marked paid. Actual settlement producer/commands remain absent until Q-75 is approved.

## Privacy owner contract

Implement `UserDataPort.exportPage({userId,cursor,limit})` for approved own payment/refund/purchase references and amounts only; never raw provider secrets, tokens, payloads, other parties' finances or full payout instruments. `erase({userId,requestId,policyVersion}) -> {receipt,status:'completed'|'blocked',reasonCode?}` respects financial retention/unfinished obligations; Q-32 unknown policy returns blocked, not successful no-op. No deletion cascade; minimize personal linkage only when approved policy permits and audit the receipt.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a multi-shop checkout, when Payments confirms every required online allocation, then checkout receives one idempotent success result and each resulting order links its own Payments-owned allocation.
2. Given the provider redirects the browser but no valid signed success event exists, when checkout refreshes, then the attempt remains pending and no order is reported as paid.
3. Given the same webhook is delivered twice, when the second delivery is processed, then payment/order events and ledger entries are not duplicated.
4. Given a webhook signature, amount, currency or attempt reference is invalid, when received, then it is rejected and no payment state changes.
5. Given an authorized cash order, when handover is confirmed, then its payment changes from pending to collected exactly once; order placement alone does not collect it.
6. Given a refund amount exceeds the remaining captured amount, when Orders requests it, then Payments rejects it without calling the provider.
7. Given a vendor requests another business's earnings or starts a purchase for it, when the business ID is altered, then the request is denied without disclosing that business's records.
8. Given an unconfigured plan, promotion, commission or settlement rule, when the vendor opens Earnings or starts a purchase, then no sample value or success-shaped fallback is shown.
9. Given an authorized, configured plan purchase and a verified successful payment, when the event is applied, then the subscription period starts only once and the effective plan is returned from the subscription record.
10. Given capture succeeds but zero orders commit, when compensation/recovery runs twice, then one return obligation per allocation is persisted without an order FK and the customer can observe pending/completed/blocked recovery.
11. Given late capture races a retry/COD fallback, when processed, then old funding is reconciled/compensated and cannot pay or place the replacement round.
12. Given approval arrives before request, duplicate decisions or delayed provider outcomes, when processed, then refund state is monotonic, provider execution occurs once, and only `payment.refund_completed` records returned money.
13. Given shared funding and concurrent refund/compensation requests, when budgets are reserved, then completed + pending returns never exceed allocation or charge funding.
14. Given a configured purchase fails, when its owner reads/retries it, then status is recoverable after reload and retry cannot double-charge, extend a period or bypass new suspension/disabled flags.
15. Given earnings/invoice/settlement policy is missing or Q-33 remains open, when read, then unknown fields/features are explicitly unavailable, not zero totals, seeded invoices, fake empty saved methods or a payout promise.
16. Given a current cash payable differs from an old job snapshot, when handover submits the old financial version, then collection fails without consuming the code; current-version collection uses the revised amount once.
17. Given an erase request lacks approved policy or has unfinished refunds, when processed, then the receipt is blocked and retained financial facts/provider secrets are not erased/exported as a workaround.
18. Given concurrent case decisions, adjustment or another return against shared funding, when `commitDecision` runs, then case decision/outbox/reserved budget commit together or all roll back; a later decision event never reserves twice. Policy-unavailable or projection-pending context cannot approve.
19. Given a business has an active Premium subscription, when Product/Service asks `featuredEligibility` at a UTC instant inside its effective interval, then Payments returns active; before start, after expiry or with unresolved subscription policy, it returns not-active/unavailable and never authorizes featured status.
20. Given a provider owns a verified finite Premium subscription, when they cancel with the current version, then cancellation is recorded for the paid expiry instant, entitlement remains active until that instant, and no refund/payment operation occurs; stale, expired, foreign, or no-expiry subscriptions are rejected without mutation.
21. Given an expiry job is replayed or a verified renewal covers the expiry boundary, when the job runs, then it emits at most one expiry event only for a real eligibility loss; a later verified renewal restores still-selected public listings and invalidates their cached featured state.

## Tests

- Unit: exact ST-payment/ST-refund transitions, integer-paise caps, current plan lookup/cancellation state, event deduplication and immutable ledger insertion.
- Integration: provider fake success/failure/timeout, signed webhook verification, checkout event replay, cash collection, refund retries, subscription expiry boundary/renewal, delayed-job replay and ledger queries with keyset pagination.
- Authorization negatives: wrong role, non-owner vendor/provider, cross-business record identifier, inactive/unapproved business purchase, invalid internal event identity; assert 403/404 and no leaked records.
- Concurrency: duplicate webhook/refund event and simultaneous refund requests cannot exceed the remaining captured amount or create duplicate entries.
- Security: signatures checked before mutation; credentials/payment details excluded from logs and responses; plan/promotion keys allow-listed.
- Map AC1–AC21 to unit/contract/database scenarios; add provider timeout-after-capture, crash-before-order, both callback orders, stale purchase retry, signature/amount/currency mismatch, unsafe money integers, same-source/different-payload replay, cancellation-at-expiry and renewal exactly at/after the boundary. Assert financial conservation across shared charge budgets, no double ledger entry, at-most-once expiry notice, IST month boundaries and no raw financial evidence in telemetry.
