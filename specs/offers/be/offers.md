# BE: offers

Features: F-OF-001, F-OF-002, F-OF-003, F-OF-004, F-OF-005, F-OF-006, F-PD-004, F-VD-007, F-AD-011; promotion activation for F-VD-017 | Rules: BR-01, BR-02, BR-06, BR-12, BR-17, BR-19 | State machines: ST-offer | Depends on: businesses, products, services, media-videos, settings; Payments purchase events | Used by: search, cart-checkout, payments, admin

Frontend counterpart: [FE offers](../fe/offers.md) · [Feature inventory](../../../docs/FEATURE_INVENTORY.md)

## Responsibility

Own offers/coupons, eligibility, vendor offer management, and **all three Promotions panels**, including homepage banners and featured-business placements. Payments owns charges, not placements; Search composes read projections, not promotion configuration. Cart/Checkout owns totals and coupon allocation.

Contract conventions: [backend-shared](../../_foundation/backend-shared.md) flat errors `{code,message_key,status,details,correlationId}`; database money is `bigint` paise, JSON money is a safe-integer paise **number**, never a bigint/string/rupee float. All mutations use CSRF, explicit DTO allow-lists and current authorization.

## Rules and use-cases

1. Require an approved owner business (BR-06). Derive `business_id` from the session; verify every product/service target belongs to that business and matches the offer scope.
2. Creation starts `pending`. Admin approval moves `pending` to `active`; rejection records `review_decision='rejected'`, ends the offer, and stores the reason. Vendor can pause/resume an approved offer or end it; `ended` is terminal. A rejected offer cannot be activated by its owner.
3. An edit requeues the updated offer as `pending` as observed in the prototype; its edited terms/discount stop applying until reapproved. This interaction with ST-offer is tracked in Q-68.
4. An offer applies only when approved/active, `valid_from <= now < valid_to`, and its target is eligible. End expired offers idempotently; derive out-of-stock state from current product inventory and never apply an unavailable target (BR-17).
5. Public lists and Home contain only active, valid offers; detail surfaces may show expired or out-of-stock states, never pending/rejected records. `featured` affects placement only after approval and does not bypass eligibility.
6. Offers supplies versioned eligibility/rules, not a client-calculated total. Cart/Checkout caps a coupon at the eligible subtotal **before** proportional allocation, including zero subtotal (BR-19). Relevant unresolved Q-62 pricing combinations block a quote instead of silently ignoring an advertised discount; Q-67 remains gated.
7. Admin review filters by the offer's owning business scope, not a client-supplied city/area. Enforce role + `promos` permission + city/area scope server-side; audit actor, timestamp, action and before/after values (BR-12).
8. Store UTC validity instants and render in IST (BR-02). Date-only boundary semantics are unresolved in Q-64; until decided, eligibility uses the persisted exclusive `valid_to` instant.
9. Offer view/use metrics are informational only; they never decide eligibility or ranking until their definitions are approved (Q-65).
10. Manual placement lifecycle: draft → active ↔ paused → ended; deletion removes it from public reads but preserves purchase/audit references. Activation requires valid scope/content, in-window schedule and an approved public target. A null manual start/end is an open bound; purchased periods require configured instants. Admin content edits, including background replacement/removal, revalidate guards; expired/ended purchases cannot be extended by a toggle. Admin authorization controls manual placement; business and existing offer/media approval still apply, no invented independent approver.
11. Paid activation consumes `business.promotion.purchased` once by purchase ID. Recheck prepared configuration, target approval and content; activate for the configured period or record `activation_blocked_reason`. Never fabricate duration, rank, price or renewal; purchasing is unavailable until Q-74 configuration is executable. A payment alone cannot bypass a suspended business, an expired window or missing content.
12. Check current Settings flags at purchase preparation (`payments_online`) and current approval on every mutation/read. Flags do not authorize a user. Suspending/removing a target immediately excludes its offers/placements even before cache invalidation; purchased-period compensation after suspension remains Q-74, not an automatic refund.
13. Q-57/Q-60's **ownership** gap is resolved here by final `js11.js:75–78` controls and the existing Payments placement boundary. Popular-business ranking and paid placement overlap/renewal policy remain questions; Search must not invent them.

### Coupon creator, scope and redemption

1. A vendor/provider coupon's creator is the authenticated owner and its eligibility scope is only that approved business. An admin coupon is platform-eligible, but its eligible businesses are constrained to the creating admin's assigned city/area; only super_admin may create without that geographic restriction. Creator IDs and scope are derived server-side, never accepted from a client DTO. Every admin read/write checks `promos:view/edit`, old and new scope, and writes the existing audit record.
2. Vendor/provider-created coupons enter `pending` and use the existing Offers moderation decision and audit flow before becoming active; editing an active owner coupon suspends that edited version and returns it to pending. Admin-created coupons enter `draft` and use the existing versioned activation/status command. Active means approved/configured, not necessarily currently usable: public eligibility additionally requires `valid_from <= now < valid_to`, current business approval/visibility and target eligibility.
3. Codes are globally unique after normalization; collision is a stable conflict, not a silent code rewrite. Require explicit `valid_from` and exclusive `valid_to` UTC instants, type/value, minimum and optional cap. Do not create sample coupon records or infer a duration.
4. `product_checkout` coupons apply only to eligible product lines/businesses; compute the minimum against the combined eligible product subtotal across the checkout, then use the existing deterministic proportional paise allocation over eligible shop subtotals only. Ineligible shops and service lines receive zero allocation. Recheck coupon/business/target versions at final quote submission.
5. `service_enquiry_display` is available only to provider-owned service targets. It may show the approved code/description and standard enquiry CTA on that service; it never enters a cart, quote, order, booking, service price, payment or discount calculation.
6. Q-67's usage limits/per-customer limits, stacking/precedence and cancellation/refund redemption treatment remain unresolved. Until decided, no coupon code is redeemable in checkout, no redemption reservation/count/event is written, and any quote with a coupon code fails closed with `CHECKOUT_COUPON_POLICY_UNAVAILABLE`. This gate does not prevent coupon authoring/moderation or enquiry-only display.

## Events and jobs

- Publish exactly `offer.submitted`, `offer.approved`, `offer.rejected`, `offer.paused`, `offer.resumed`, `offer.ended`, `offer.expired`; payload `{offerId,businessId,version,targetIds,reasonKey?}` plus the [commerce event envelope](../../orders/be/orders.md#event-contracts).
- Publish `promotion.placement_changed` for create/edit/activate/pause/end/delete/blocked activation and `promotion.placement_expired` for expiry; payload `{placementId,purchaseId?,businessId?,kind,scope,fromStatus?,toStatus,version,startsAt?,endsAt?,reasonKey?}`. Banner-asset changes invalidate the same Home/placement tags; Search/public caches revalidate Home, placement and target tags; Payments observes activation via the typed read port.
- Publish `coupon.submitted`, `coupon.approved`, `coupon.rejected`, `coupon.changed`, `coupon.paused`, `coupon.resumed`, `coupon.ended`, and `coupon.expired` with coupon/business/scope/version IDs only. They invalidate Offers/Services and checkout eligibility caches; approval events do not imply a redemption or send marketing. `coupon.expire-due` is idempotent and uses the exclusive UTC `valid_to`.
- Consume Payments' `business.promotion.purchased {purchaseId,paymentAttemptId,businessId,promotionKey,placementId,configurationVersion,period,amountPaise}`; no browser callback activates a placement. Duplicate/out-of-order purchase delivery cannot revive a deleted/ended placement or restart its period.
- `checkout.coupon_committed` remains a future contract only after Q-67 redemption/usage policy is decided; until then no coupon redemption event or use count is emitted. `offers.expire-due` / `promotions.expire-due` recheck time/version and emit their exact expiry event once; reads also enforce `now < endsAt/validTo`, so queue delay cannot keep an expired discount/placement live.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given an approved owner submits a valid offer, when creation succeeds, then it is pending/excluded from public reads and one `offer.submitted` event is persisted, not premature approval.
- Given an offer target belongs to another business or conflicts with `scope`, when create/update is requested, then validation rejects it without changing either business's data.
- Given an admin with `promos:edit` and matching area scope approves a pending offer, when the decision is saved, then it becomes active only within its validity window and before/after are audit-recorded.
- Given an admin lacks `promos:edit` or is outside the offer business scope, when the decision is attempted, then it is denied and status remains unchanged.
- Given an offer is paused, ended, expired, pending or rejected, when public discovery or checkout reads it, then it is not applied; expired/out-of-stock public detail has its distinct state.
- Given a vendor edits an active offer, when the versioned update succeeds, then the edited offer returns to pending and no longer applies before approval.
- Given coupon code case differs from storage, when cart-checkout validates it, then normalized lookup returns the same eligibility result; invalid/inactive coupons cannot change the quote.
- Given a vendor/provider creates a coupon, when saved, then creator/business scope comes from the session and it is pending moderation; a different-area admin or foreign owner cannot read/edit/approve it.
- Given an authorized admin creates a coupon, when saved, then it is a draft with server-bound city/area eligibility; out-of-scope businesses cannot be selected, edited or activated.
- Given a service enquiry coupon is approved and currently valid, when the targeted public service loads, then its informational coupon appears with the standard enquiry action and no price/payment/booking mutation; another service or hidden provider does not show it.
- Given a product coupon is active or its code is entered at checkout before Q-67 closes, when pricing resolves, then the quote fails with `CHECKOUT_COUPON_POLICY_UNAVAILABLE`; no discount, redemption reservation, use count or committed event is written.
- Given an owner edits an active coupon or simultaneous creators claim the same normalized code, when commands race, then the old owner coupon is not live during moderation and only one globally unique code write succeeds.
- Given expiration or approval events are retried, when the job/consumer repeats, then status, counters and notifications are not applied twice.
- Given a product is opened without offer context after expiry, when target-state is read, then the previously approved offer is returned as expired with regular price; no pending/rejected data leaks.
- Given an authorized scoped admin adds, edits, toggles or deletes a banner/featured placement, when its versioned command commits, then only eligible scoped public reads change and before/after is audited.
- Given an authorized scoped admin uploads, replaces or removes a homepage banner background, when the versioned placement command commits, then the existing `banner_asset_id` changes once, audit/outbox are written, and Home revalidates without creating a second banner system.
- Given a banner has no image or its image is unavailable, when Home renders it, then the existing text-only banner remains usable; when an image exists, then responsive cropping uses the existing token-backed banner treatment and localized text remains WCAG-readable.
- Given the same paid-promotion event is delivered twice or after expiry/deletion, when Offers handles it, then at most one placement/period exists and no stale event reactivates it.
- Given activation policy/content is missing, payments are disabled, or the business is suspended, when a purchase is prepared, then purchasing is unavailable and no charge is initiated.
- Given an asset belongs to another business, is private KYC, or a destination contains a script/external URL, when submitted, then validation rejects it without publishing.
