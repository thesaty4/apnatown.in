## Responsibility

Own per-shop orders, immutable purchase/fulfilment revisions, stock allocation/operation records, lifecycle commands, cases/refund decision handoff, and order invoices. Delivery owns jobs/codes, Payments owns funds/refund execution. No arbitrary status setter or direct cross-module repository access.

Use [foundation](../../_foundation/backend-shared.md) flat errors `{code,message_key,status,details,correlationId}`, CSRF/session guards and allow-listed DTOs. Money is `bigint` paise in storage and safe-integer paise **numbers** in JSON; never strings/float rupees. Admin authorization always combines module permission and authoritative city **and area** scope.

## Rules and use-cases

Creation/eligible zero-after-adjustment calls [Payments' no-charge settlement port](../../payments/be/payments.md#zero-payable-order-settlement) in the same UoW. Order/payment DTOs expose `settlementKind` and `fundingSatisfied`; status alone does not prove positive customer funding. Zero-charge cancellation/return creates applicable immutable fiscal/subsidy reversals, never a zero refund request or fake payment-refunded event. Existing return/transition authority is unchanged.

1. Preserve one order per shop (BR-03). Validate pinned quote/address revision and current payment round; stock operations, orders/history, payment links, one master summary plus its shop links, and checkout commit share one UoW. No order/stock/summary write survives a failed shop; captured-but-unplaced compensation is Payments/Checkout-owned and needs no fake order. Provider product orders require explicitly configured fulfilment: null delivery method/disabled pickup is unavailable, never an inferred free/rider/pickup mode.
2. Store purchase-time product names, variant, quantities, paise prices, discount allocation, fees and business details. Later catalog/profile/address edits cannot rewrite an existing order or invoice. Money uses integer paise; times use UTC, display IST (BR-01/02).
3. Explicit handlers keyed `(mode,currentStatus,event)` lock the order and compare expected version, live approval and caller ownership. Commands `accept:placed→accepted` (both modes), `start_preparing:accepted→preparing` (delivery), `mark_ready:preparing→ready` (delivery) / `accepted→ready` (pickup) are the business progression commands. Key/hash replay cannot duplicate history/notifications; a changed payload or losing version conflicts.
4. Delivery ready → out_for_delivery → delivered and pickup ready → picked_up require the verified map below. Refunded follows a **full verified** `payment.refund_completed` from delivered/picked_up/cancelled, never approval or a partial adjustment. No generic admin override; wrong-mode/invalid edges fail without side effects.
5. Customer cancellation permits delivery placed/accepted/preparing and pickup placed/accepted; manual vendor reject is placed-only. Reason/key/version mandatory. Internal all-items-unavailable cancellation uses those pre-fulfilment states. Release only outstanding allocations, never already released lines. Captured cancellation opens a refund request/case and stays cancelled until verified refund completion.
6. Customer refund request requires delivered and `now < fulfilledAt + configuredWindow` (prototype 24 hours; Settings production value). Boundary uses server UTC instants, displayed IST. Pickup refund requests stay disabled Q-79. Policy-derived amount may remain unavailable/requested, never client-specified or auto-approved. Notify vendor/admin on accepted cancellation/refund once (BR-20).
7. Case open → in_review → resolved; admin create/note/status needs `orders:edit` + exact city/area scope and audit. Rider intake creates a case through Delivery's verified job link; jobless dispute uses Settings support. Until Q-78, customers/riders see permitted case status, not staff notes/evidence; notes cannot smuggle visibility or refund authority.
8. Partial fulfilment is **one adjustment transaction**: lock order/items/stock allocations and financial revision; calculate immutable before/after snapshots; claim adjustment/release IDs; call Products release and Payments `applyOrderAdjustment`; persist item/financial revisions and `order.items_adjusted`. No deletion of ordered lines or second stock deduction. Failed policy/payment/stock guard rolls back everything.
9. For eligible unpaid cash removal, current payable is the approved remaining-line bill, not the original COD snapshot. Remaining line nets derive from ordered allocations; coupon requalification/fee redistribution requires approved Q-77 policy. Simple removal without ambiguous discount/fee policy may proceed; otherwise `ORDER_ADJUSTMENT_POLICY_UNAVAILABLE`. **Paid/collected partial action stays disabled until Q-76/Q-77 defines refund amount, fees and approval**; do not remove stock/items first and defer the money gap.
10. Eventual paid adjustment must link one refund request by `adjustmentId`, expose ordered total / fulfilled total / current payable / funded / returned / refund pending separately, and leave issued snapshots intact. Full unavailability cancels/releases only outstanding stock once; refund policy stays gated. Cancellation after a prior removal releases remaining lines, not the original quantity. Monetary refund alone never restores stock.
11. Orders writes history/outbox in that UoW; event replay sees the same key and is a no-op. No code or raw rider phone enters an order/event DTO.

Customer active combines the first three. Waiting-for-rider count uses Delivery's typed assignment projection for ready platform orders, never a new order status.

Canonical `AssetPort.upload` returns only synchronously validated/durable ready assets; attachment rechecks via `AssetPort.validateAttachment`. No pending-upload reference/state is persisted here. Reject public URLs, unregistered/foreign assets and KYC-purpose assets. Limits/retention Q-80 remain gated; optional evidence never bypasses validation.
15. Refund decision authorization is unresolved Q-17, including vendor vs admin authority. Until approved, disable decision actions. Otherwise lock the linked case/expected case version, call `PaymentsRefundPort.decisionContext` and `commitDecision` with its refund/financial versions, then append one case decision/audit and `refund.approved`/`refund.declined` in the same UoW. Approval reserves server-derived funds atomically; concurrent notes/decisions or exhausted budget conflict without half-decisions. No `refund.decision_requested` intermediate or manual provider completion. Authorized decline may escalate only under configured source policy, not silently approve.
16. Create one stable master checkout summary only after the complete per-shop order set commits; the unique checkout attempt and creation replay return the same document. Its original totals/line allocations are immutable. Current cancellation, fulfilled amount and actual refund status are separately composed from owning order revisions and verified Payments outcomes. It is not a legal tax invoice and links to issued supplier documents; customer payable reconciles with platform-funded contributions as specified below.
17. Customer can view/print only their own checkout summary; vendors/providers can view only their own per-shop order/invoice copy and cannot fetch the master summary. Admin read requires `orders:view` and authoritative city/area scope. Summary and invoices are private no-store; unavailable legal invoices are marked unavailable without blocking the summary.

### Invoice and tax policy

**Decision 2026-11-11: Q-06's implementation contract is resolved, not a claim that any seller is tax-registered or legally verified.** This is a marketplace: the business supplies its goods and self-delivery; ApnaTown supplies its platform fee, platform-arranged delivery and paid plans/promotions/commission services. Supplier agreements and verified fiscal configuration must support those roles before the corresponding charge is enabled. Do not invent GSTINs, registrations, HSN/SAC codes, rates, legal names or e-invoice applicability.

| Rule | Implementation contract |
|---|---|
| Fiscal inputs | Businesses owns verified `SupplierTaxProfile`; Products selects a configured `taxCode`; Settings owns classifications/platform supplier. `SupplierTaxProfile={supplierRef,legalName,address,stateCode,registrationMode:'regular'\|'composition'\|'exempt'\|'unregistered',gstin?,requiresIrn,authorizedSignatoryRef,version}`. Registered modes require verified GSTIN/state. Tax classification includes `supplyType:'taxable'\|'exempt'\|'nil_rated'`, HSN/SAC, unit and rates; exemptions require zero configured tax. Missing data returns `ORDER_TAX_CONFIGURATION_REQUIRED` before payment, never a zero-tax fallback. |
| Document types | Regular taxable supply: tax invoice; legally eligible composition or registered exempt/nil-rated supply: bill of supply; unregistered supply: commercial receipt labelled not a GST invoice. Mixed taxable/exempt baskets split documents by supplier/type, without a second tax engine. No GST is collected for composition/exempt/unregistered supply. Customer goods checkout is B2C; plan/commission billing uses the business recipient's verified identity. |
| Supplier separation | Each order has a shop document for merchandise + self-delivery and, when nonzero, a separate platform document for platform fee + platform-arranged delivery. Never print platform fees as the shop's taxable supply. Vendor access includes only documents linked to that vendor's order; customers only their checkout/order; admins require `orders:view` and every included order's exact scope. No partial-scope master summary may reveal sibling shops. |
| Tax extraction | Prices/fees are tax-inclusive. Merchant offers/coupons reduce supplier consideration; platform coupons are third-party funding, not seller discounts. For taxable supply extract tax with integer half-up `grossAfterMerchantDiscount * totalRateBps / (10000 + totalRateBps)`; base is residual. Split configured components with deterministic largest-remainder paise allocation; same-state CGST + SGST/UTGST, interstate IGST, configured cess once. Checkout resolves delivery state through `GeoTaxPort.stateForCity` using the authoritative address, pickup through supplier state, and freezes it. Billing recipient state comes from verified business identity. Unsupported fiscal cases are unavailable, never inferred from IP/client locale. |
| Funding reconciliation | Persist per-line `offerDiscountPaise,merchantCouponPaise,platformCouponPaise,taxSnapshot` and fee supplier/tax snapshots with Orders' immutable purchase revision. Sum supplier-document gross = customer `ordered_total_paise` + platform coupon contribution. Master summary shows the contribution as funding and subtracts it once to reach customer payable; a tax invoice may therefore exceed the customer's cash share. Payments records the platform contribution as a liability/campaign expense, not captured customer money or seller discount. |
| Invoice identity | Shared immutable `InvoiceSnapshot={id,kind,number,series,financialYear,issuedAt,supplier,recipient,placeOfSupplyStateCode,reverseCharge,lines:[{sourceRef,description,hsnOrSac,unitCode,quantity,supplyType,grossPaise,merchantDiscountPaise,taxablePaise,cgstPaise,sgstPaise,utgstPaise,igstPaise,cessPaise,totalPaise}],totals,originalDocumentId?,irn?,signingEvidence?}`. `totals` sums the same monetary fields once; exemption bases remain separately identifiable by supplyType. Fiscal facts survive listing/account edits. No gateway receipt is a tax invoice. |
| Numbering | Financial year is Apr 1–Mar 31 IST. Use module-owned locked counters per supplier/FY/series; order series `O`, credit `OC`, purchase/commission series `P`, credit `PC`. Format `{series}/{FY-start-YY}/{9-digit-sequence}`, <=16 characters, unique per supplier/FY/series. UUID is the API identity; legal numbers need not be globally unique across suppliers. Replays reuse a number; never recycle an issued/voided number. |
| Issue timing | On committed order creation, queue invoice issuance using the frozen fiscal snapshot and stable source key. Packing slips/master summaries remain available while invoice generation is pending; `mark_ready` requires current supplier documents issued. A required IRN/signature comes from a configured compliant invoicing adapter before `issued`; failures stay pending/failed with bounded retry, never fabricated evidence. Pure PDF rendering does not allocate a new number. Plan/promotion invoices are issued after verified purchase; commission invoices after recognition, in Payments. |
| Corrections/refunds | Approved cancellation or line removal creates a linked immutable credit document for the exact originally allocated supplier/tax amounts; cumulative credit <= original document amounts. No re-pricing or new tax rate. Customer refund = credited gross minus reversed platform contribution for those lines; refund subsidy to the platform liability, not customer cash. Requested/declined refunds alone issue no credit. Credit issuance and actual cash return are separate statuses. Existing refund authority/return-eligibility rules still apply; this policy never authorizes a payment. |

Orders owns `order_billing_documents(id PK,order_id FK,supplier_ref,kind,series,financial_year,sequence,source_key UNIQUE,financial_version,snapshot jsonb,original_document_id?,status CHECK pending/issued/failed,created_at/issued_at)` plus `order_invoice_counters(supplier_ref,financial_year,series,next_sequence; composite PK)`. UNIQUE `(supplier_ref,financial_year,series,sequence)` and `(order_id,supplier_ref,kind,financial_version)` prevent duplicate issue; `(order_id,created_at,id)` serves private document reads. Persist the snapshot/number claim and issuance outbox atomically; external signing/IRN I/O is outside the UoW. A credit arriving before invoice issue waits for that original source; it cannot disappear or rewrite the original.

`GET /orders/{orderId}/invoice` returns `OrderInvoiceDTO={orderId,documents:[{id,kind,number?,status,reasonCode?,viewPath?}],funding:{customerPayablePaise,platformContributionPaise}}`; protected `GET /orders/{orderId}/invoice/{documentId}` renders only an issued, linked authorized snapshot, no-store/noindex. No signed URL is stored in the document. `ORDER_INVOICE_PENDING` (409), `ORDER_TAX_CONFIGURATION_REQUIRED` (409), and `ORDER_DOCUMENT_NOT_FOUND` (404) use `errors.orders.*` keys. Invoice status is never a paid-status assertion.

Forward migration adds document/counter and fiscal/funding records. Legacy `orders.invoice_number/invoice_snapshot` become read-only migration inputs, not new issuance fields: drop their global number uniqueness after verified import; new reads/writes use document records exclusively. Unknown historical tax stays `legacy_unverified`, never inferred from today's rates. Add that state to document read/storage status. New issuance requires configuration. Add immutable `orders.primary_category_id_snapshot uuid NULL FK categories` for reporting, populated from the approved business at placement; unverifiable legacy rows are null/Unclassified.
Master snapshots additionally include per-shop and total `{supplierDocumentTotalPaise,platformContributionPaise}`; enforce supplier total minus platform contribution equals original customer payable. Items/fees carry the funding/tax snapshot defined above. These are source projections, not another tax calculation.

Acceptance: Given a platform-funded 100-paise coupon against a 1000-paise merchant invoice, when checkout is placed, then customer payment is 900, merchant consideration is 1000 and platform contribution is 100, without tax/charge duplication. Given a 11800-paise inclusive line at a **test-only** 1800-bps rate, then taxable base is 10000 and tax 1800. Given retries, concurrent FY rollover, mixed supplier fees, unregistered sellers, foreign order IDs, or a delayed IRN, then numbers/snapshots remain correct and no forged tax invoice is served. Given a credited line, then original tax components and customer/subsidy shares reconcile exactly.

Reference: [CBIC tax-invoice particulars](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-invoice-rules.html). Legal/tax operators validate current registration, classifications, signing and e-invoice obligations before production; configuration readiness is a deployment check, not an unanswered algorithm.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a two-shop checkout, when `createPerShopOrders` commits, then exactly two independent orders and their purchase snapshots exist; replaying the same key returns the same IDs.
2. Given a transition is not allowed for the order mode/current status, when requested, then the service returns `ORDERS_INVALID_TRANSITION` and writes no history, stock or notification side effect.
3. Given two actors race to update one order version, when both submit the same expected version, then exactly one transition commits.
4. Given a customer cancels an eligible unpaid order, when they submit a reason, then it becomes cancelled, stock is restored once, and vendor/admin notifications are queued.
5. Given a captured order is cancelled or refunded, when the request is accepted, then Payments receives one server-derived refund request and the order is not marked refunded until Payments confirms completion.
6. Given an authorized unpaid adjustment with configured amount policy, when an item is unavailable, then stock is released once, immutable ordered lines remain, fulfilled/current payable amounts reconcile and the customer is notified; no replacement item is inserted.
7. Given a customer or business requests another party's order, or an admin's area is outside scope, when accessed, then the request returns 404/403 without leaking order, address or phone data.
8. Given an admin without `orders:edit` or within the wrong area attempts to update a case, when submitted, then authorization fails and no audit/state change is written.
9. Given an order is delivered, when the owner opens its invoice, then the printable document uses its immutable item and amount snapshot; no unconfigured tax amount is invented.
10. Given a customer opens an order, when it is returned, then tracking summary contains no raw OTP/pickup code; code delivery is handled only through the approved Delivery customer channel.
11. Given a case contains internal notes or evidence, when a customer reads the order, then only case status is returned until Q-78 explicitly approves customer-visible updates or attachments.
12. Given an approved owner submits accept/prepare/ready for the correct mode, when the versioned idempotent command succeeds, then DTO/history/event advances once; wrong actor/mode/stale version cannot advance it.
13. Given paid partial fulfilment lacks Q-76/Q-77 policy, when requested, then action is disabled/rejected and **no** bill, stock, refund or notification side effect occurs.
14. Given one item was already restored, when cancellation races another adjustment, then released quantity never exceeds reserved quantity and current COD payable cannot use the original total.
15. Given platform/self/pickup handover events are replayed or arrive after cancellation, when applied, then only the exact guarded map may advance the order; no fake job, duplicate cash collection or code exposure occurs.
16. Given delivery fails after pickup, when the source-confirmed exception runs, then order is cancelled with failed-delivery case/refund request as applicable, but stock is held rather than restocked without return evidence.
17. Given an admin opens a case or assigned rider reports a dispute, when authorized intake/notes commit, then an actual case/status reference exists and replay creates one case; same-city/wrong-area staff and foreign riders are denied.
18. Given owner tabs/overview are read, when pagination/filtering occurs, then all five counts share list ownership predicates, while period metrics use half-open IST periods.
19. Given a two-shop checkout commits or its placement command replays, when its summary is read, then exactly one immutable master summary links both shop orders and totals equal their per-shop allocations without double-counting discounts or fees.
20. Given a shop legal invoice is unavailable, when the customer opens/prints the master summary, then it remains available as a non-tax summary and clearly shows the invoice unavailable. Given an issued shop invoice exists, then its link opens only that shop's immutable legal invoice; no master tax amount is invented.
21. Given an order is cancelled, adjusted or refunded, when the master summary is reopened, then original totals remain unchanged and current fulfilled/cancelled/verified-returned amounts are separately reconciled; requested or pending refund is not shown as returned.
22. Given a vendor requests another shop's invoice or any vendor requests the master summary, or an admin outside assigned scope requests either, when accessed, then no document or linked shop data is disclosed.
19. Given refund decisions/outcomes are duplicated/reordered, when reconciled, then one authorized decision/execution occurs, approval is not completion, and partial completion cannot refund the whole order.
20. Given unknown privacy policy or unfinished accounting obligations, when erase/export runs, then restricted fields/financial facts are retained appropriately and a blocked receipt cannot masquerade as completion.
